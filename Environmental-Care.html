Sustainability In Action

Pioneers in Germany in the Use of Renewable Energy

Wind Power & Clean Energy

We have installed two Vestas 1.8 MW wind turbines, generating the electricity needed for our entire production. Therefore, we can assert that our mills run using 98% of renewable energy.

Effluent Treatment Plant

Located in a rural area outside of Bremen surrounded by 100 hectares of forested land, our modern effluent treatment plant ensures all liquid and solid effluents meet the highest government environmental standards.

Tree Plantation & CO2 Offset

We have planted 10,000 trees at our facilities, irrigated by treated effluent waters. They contribute directly to water recycling while actively absorbing CO2 from the atmosphere.
