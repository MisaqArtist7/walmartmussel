Our Values

At Walmart Mussel, our values are the foundation of our business. We are committed to honesty, loyalty, quality, and responsible resource use, ensuring that we maintain strong relationships with our clients, employees, and suppliers while delivering exceptional products.

Our Purpose & Future

Mission & Vision

Excellence & Differentiation

To differentiate among the wool industry due to the service we offer our customers, always paying close attention to detail and assuring total transparency and trust.

To stand out for the passion that we have towards wool fibre and to elaborate optimum quality products, always addressing each one of our client’s specific needs.

Innovation & Heritage

To aim to a continuous improvement of our products and services, beginning every day with an open mind to new ideas.

Combining the experience of 65 years in the wool business with the freshness of a new endeavour.

Community & Sustainability

To fully integrate into the Fray Marcos community, where our employees live, and to constantly search for new strategies in order to preserve our environment.

Respecting the Craft

To try to attain the new challenges that we take on every day, always taking care of the attributes of this beautiful fibre with which we work on a daily basis and respecting the work of all the growers that trust us with their products.

"When you choose to work with us, you are choosing a company that combines the experience and tradition of a family business with the flexibility and dynamism of a modern endeavour."

Federico Mussel

Managing Director
